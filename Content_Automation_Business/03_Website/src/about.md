**Band of One** is for people who run a service business on their own: freelancers, consultants, coaches, virtual assistants, designers, developers and writers.

When you're a business of one, you're also the receptionist, project manager, account manager and collections department. Most advice about that work is either an AI-tool listicle or a 10,000-prompt bundle. We think there's a better way: **complete, tested workflows** with templates you can copy, and plain talk about what AI should *not* do.

## What we publish

- **[Client Workflows](/category/client-workflows/):** onboarding, proposals, weekly updates, scope
- **[Getting Paid](/category/getting-paid/):** invoices, deposits, payment terms, reminders
- **[AI, Done Right](/category/ai-done-right/):** prompts with guardrails, privacy, sounding like yourself
- **[Systems & SOPs](/category/systems-sops/):** SOPs, weekly reviews, what to automate
- **[Tools, Honestly](/category/tools-honestly/):** comparisons labeled as tested or researched

## How we work

- Every article solves one job and includes something usable.
- Facts are sourced and dated. Tool reviews say whether we tested the tool or researched it.
- We use AI to help research and draft, and a human reviews every article against a written checklist before it's published. See our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).
- We don't publish fake testimonials, income claims or invented case studies. Examples are clearly marked as fictional.

## How we make money

We sell a small set of [digital products](/resources/) built on the same system as the articles. In the future we may join affiliate programs for tools we recommend. If we do, affiliate links will be clearly labeled, and they never decide what we recommend. See our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
