We write for people who run a service business alone, and whose time is scarce. That shapes every rule below.

## Every article must

- **Solve one specific job** and include something usable: a template, checklist, prompt or worked example.
- **Source its facts.** Statistics include who measured them, how, and when. Sources are listed at the end of each article with the date we checked them.
- **Separate fact from opinion.** Recommendations are clearly our judgment.
- **Mark fictional examples as fictional.** "Sam, a fictional web designer" is an illustration, not a case study.

## Tested vs researched

Every article that reviews or compares tools carries one of two labels:

- <span class="badge tested">Tested</span> **Tested:** we used it hands-on for the purpose described, and we say how and when. Example: our invoice-tracking formulas were recalculated against eight known scenarios before publishing.
- <span class="badge researched">Researched</span> **Researched:** based on the provider's official documentation and pricing pages, checked on the date shown. We haven't necessarily used every feature ourselves.

We never claim hands-on experience we don't have, and we never say "we tested 50 tools".

## How we use AI

We use AI assistants to help with research, outlining and first drafts, the same way we suggest our readers use them. **A human reviews every article before publication** against a checklist: facts and dates checked against sources, claims supported, examples marked, links working, no invented quotes, results or experiences.

## What we won't publish

- Fake testimonials, reviews or case studies
- Income, earnings or guaranteed-results claims
- Invented statistics or search volumes
- Legal, tax or financial advice presented as fact. Where topics touch these areas, we explain the general idea and point you to official sources and professionals.

## Updates and corrections

Prices and features change. We re-check tool facts regularly and show an "Updated" date on each article. If you spot an error, please [contact us](/contact/). We correct mistakes promptly and don't quietly rewrite history on significant changes.

## Money and independence

We sell our own [products](/resources/), and articles may mention them where they're relevant. If we join affiliate programs in future, links will be labeled and disclosed (see our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/)). Payments never buy coverage, rankings or positive reviews.
