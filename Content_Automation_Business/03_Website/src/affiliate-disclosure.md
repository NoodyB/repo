**Current status (24 September 2026): this site contains no affiliate links.** We haven't joined any affiliate programs yet. Links to tools go directly to the tool's own website.

If that changes, here's how we'll handle it.

## If an article contains affiliate links

- A **disclosure appears at the top** of the article, before the first link: *"This article contains affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes our recommendations."*
- Each affiliate link is **labeled "(affiliate link)"** and marked for search engines with `rel="sponsored"`.
- We only recommend tools we would recommend without a commission, and we say whether we've **tested** them or **researched** them.
- A commission never decides a ranking. Where a free option is the better choice, we say so.

## Our own products

We sell digital products (guides, spreadsheets, prompt libraries). When an article mentions one, that's a product we make and profit from, and we present it that way, not as an independent recommendation.

## Sponsorships

We don't currently accept sponsored content. If we ever do, it will be clearly labeled "Sponsored" at the top, and sponsors won't approve or edit our opinions.

## Why we disclose

The U.S. Federal Trade Commission expects clear and conspicuous disclosure of material connections, such as affiliate commissions. See the FTC's guidance, ["FTC's Endorsement Guides: What People Are Asking"](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking). We also think readers simply deserve to know.
