## Description

**The whole system, in one download.**

The three Band of One products are designed to work together:

1. **The Solo Admin Playbook** (38-page PDF + DOCX): *how* to set up your admin system: the audit, safe AI use, six client workflows, SOPs and a weekly review.
2. **The Client Desk** (Excel/Google Sheets workbook + guide): *where* it lives: clients, projects, invoices, reminder stages and a dashboard that shows what needs attention.
3. **The Band of One Prompt Library** (100 prompts; PDF, spreadsheet, CSV): *what to say*: fully specified prompts for inquiries, proposals, onboarding, updates, invoices, testimonials and tough conversations.

**How they connect**

- The playbook's workflows reference prompt IDs from the library (e.g., PROP-01, PAY-02).
- The Client Desk's Prompt Log uses the same IDs, and its reminder stages match the playbook's payment workflow.
- The weekly review appears in all three: the agenda (playbook), the live numbers (Client Desk) and the review assistant prompt (OPS-05).
