## Description

**Admin shouldn't be the job you do after the job.**

If you're a freelancer, consultant, coach or VA, you're also the receptionist, project manager, account manager and collections department. *The Solo Admin Playbook* shows you how to build a simple, AI-assisted system for that work, one workflow at a time, without buying new software.

**What you'll build**

- **An admin audit** that scores your recurring tasks, so you fix the ones that cost you most first
- **A safe AI workspace**: what to paste, what to redact, and which settings to check before using AI with client work
- **Six client workflows**, each with steps, templates, prompts and a review checklist:
  1. Inquiries and discovery calls
  2. Proposals that don't sound generic
  3. Onboarding in 30 minutes
  4. Delivery communication (weekly updates, feedback, scope changes)
  5. Getting paid (clear invoices and a five-stage reminder schedule)
  6. Offboarding, testimonials and referrals
- **An SOP library** so you stop re-deciding how you do things
- **Light automation** using tools you probably already have
- **A 45-minute weekly admin review** that keeps it all running

**What's inside the download**

- The Solo Admin Playbook, 38-page PDF (read on screen or print)
- Editable DOCX version: copy the templates and worksheets into Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice
- START-HERE guide and plain-language license

**How it's different**

- **Systems, not volume.** Six workflows, done properly, instead of a thousand generic tips.
- **You stay in control.** Every prompt tells the AI not to invent prices, dates or promises, and every workflow ends with a human review step.
- **Tool-agnostic.** Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot, including free tiers.
- **Sourced.** Claims about late payments, admin time and AI use cite their sources.

**Who it's for:** solo service providers (freelancers, consultants, coaches, virtual assistants, designers, developers, writers) who handle their own client admin.

**Who it's not for:** teams that need a full CRM, or anyone looking for legal, tax or accounting advice. The playbook covers communication and systems, not law.

## FAQ

**Do I need a paid AI subscription?**
No. The prompts work in free tiers of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot. Paid tiers can handle longer inputs.

**Is it safe to use AI with client information?**
Chapter 3 covers this: redact names and confidential details before pasting, and check your assistant's data and training settings. The playbook never asks you to paste passwords or financial account details.

**Will this work for my kind of business?**
It's written for one-person service businesses. The example is a web designer, but the workflows (inquiries, proposals, onboarding, delivery, payment, offboarding) apply to most client work.

**Is there legal or tax advice in here?**
No. Where the playbook touches contracts, late fees or payment terms, it gives communication templates and tells you to check local rules with a professional.

**Can I edit the templates?**
Yes. That's what the DOCX version is for.
