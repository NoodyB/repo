**One hundred prompts that do a specific job, not ten thousand that don't.**

Most prompt packs are long lists of one-liners. This library is the opposite: 100 prompts written for the admin of a one-person service business. Each one is fully specified, so you get a usable draft instead of generic text.

**Every prompt includes**

## FAQ

**How is this different from big prompt bundles?**
Each prompt is built for one admin task, with defined inputs, an output format and guardrails. There are fewer of them, and each does more.

**Do I need a paid AI plan?**
No. Free tiers work. Longer inputs, such as call transcripts, may need a paid tier depending on the assistant.

**Can I import it into Notion?**
Yes. In Notion choose **Import → CSV** and select the included CSV file.

**Is it safe to paste client information?**
The library includes a redaction prompt (SET-03) and asks you to replace names and confidential details with placeholders. Check your assistant's data settings too.

**Can I share the prompts with my team or clients?**
You can use everything you *create* with them however you like. The library itself is licensed to one business. Please don't share the files.

**What if it isn't useful for me?**
Email within 14 days for a full refund.
