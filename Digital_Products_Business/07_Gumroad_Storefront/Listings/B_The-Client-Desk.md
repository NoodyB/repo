## Summary

One spreadsheet for clients, projects, invoices and payment reminders, with a dashboard that shows what needs attention this week. Works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice.

## Description

**Know what needs doing without opening six apps.**

*The Client Desk* is a single spreadsheet that runs the admin side of a one-person service business. Type in your clients, projects and invoices. The workbook works out what's due, what's overdue, and which payment reminder comes next.

**The 10 tabs**

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| Start Here | Setup steps and the color key |
| Dashboard | Follow-ups due, invoices needing a reminder, pipeline by stage, this week's numbers |
| Clients & Pipeline | Stage, value, win probability, next action and date for every client or lead |
| Projects | Milestones, deposits and weekly update days |
| Invoices & Follow-ups | Due dates, days overdue and the next reminder stage (A–E) with the date it's due |
| Admin Audit | Score your recurring admin tasks and see which to fix first |
| Prompt Log | The AI prompts you actually use (IDs match the Band of One Prompt Library) |
| SOP Library | Your procedures and when each is due for review |
| Weekly Review | A 45-minute agenda with live numbers and a weekly log |
| Settings | Business name, payment terms, currency label, dropdown lists, reminder timing |

**Built to be trusted**

- **Tested.** Every formula was recalculated and checked against expected values by an automated test (over 60 checks) before release.
- **Portable.** Uses only widely supported functions, so it behaves the same in Excel (2010 or later), Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros, add-ons or scripts.
- **Clear.** Yellow cells are for typing, grey cells calculate. A fictional sample business shows how everything works, and you clear it in a few minutes.
- **Documented.** A 13-page user guide covers setup, every tab and troubleshooting.

**What's inside the download**

- The Client Desk workbook (.xlsx)

**Who it's for:** freelancers, consultants, coaches and VAs with roughly up to 150 clients or leads a year who want one simple place for client admin.

**Who it's not for:** teams that need shared permissions, automatic invoicing or bank feeds. Use dedicated software for those. Apple Numbers hasn't been tested.

*The Client Desk tracks and reminds; it doesn't send emails or invoices for you, and it isn't accounting software.*

## FAQ

**Does it work in Google Sheets?**
Yes. Upload the file to Google Drive, open it, then choose **File → Save as Google Sheets**. The user guide walks you through it.

**Does it send reminders or invoices automatically?**
No. It tells you which reminder is due and when. You send it with your own email or invoicing tool. The *Solo Admin Playbook* and *Prompt Library* include reminder templates.

**Can I change the reminder timing or dropdown lists?**
Yes, on the Settings tab.

**What currency does it use?**
Any. You set a currency label in Settings, and amounts are plain numbers.

**What happens if I delete a row by accident?**
Copy a grey cell from a row above and paste it into the broken row. Formulas adjust automatically. The user guide's Troubleshooting section walks through it.

**Can I use it for a team?**
The license covers one business. It's designed for one person, and spreadsheet sharing is up to your own tool (for example, Google Sheets sharing).

**What if it isn't useful for me?**
Email within 14 days for a full refund.
