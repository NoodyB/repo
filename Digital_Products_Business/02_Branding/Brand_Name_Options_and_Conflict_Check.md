# Brand Name Options & Preliminary Conflict Check

> **Important:** This is a **preliminary** screen for obvious conflicts: web search, a trademark database search (Justia Trademarks, which mirrors USPTO filings), and a live check of the obvious domain. It is **not** legal clearance. Before you spend money on a domain, print materials or a trademark filing, run a USPTO search yourself (https://tmsearch.uspto.gov) and consider a trademark attorney if you plan to register the mark.

## Selected working name: **Band of One**

**Meaning:** A one-person business is a "band of one": you play every instrument (sales, delivery, admin, marketing). The brand promises systems that help one person sound like a whole, well-rehearsed band.

**Tagline:** *Practical AI systems for businesses of one.*

**Why it won:**
- Memorable, human and gender-neutral (a twist on "one-man band").
- It does not sound like a generic AI tool, which helps credibility.
- It works for both businesses: products ("the Band of One Prompt Library") and content ("Band of One: field notes for businesses of one").
- No business, software or education conflicts found in our checks (details below).

## Decisions that need your approval

1. **Approve "Band of One"** as the business name (or pick a backup).
2. **Choose and buy a domain.** Suggested order to check: `bandofone.co`, `bandofonehq.com`, `thebandofone.com`, `bandof.one`. Prices vary by registrar and extension, and renewal prices are often higher than first-year promos. Our launch budget reserves **up to $35/year** as an *estimate*; confirm the actual first-year and renewal price before buying.
3. **Reserve social handles:** try `@bandofonehq` everywhere for consistency (`@bandofone` is taken on Instagram by a musician).
4. **Optional:** a USPTO knockout search before any paid branding work.
