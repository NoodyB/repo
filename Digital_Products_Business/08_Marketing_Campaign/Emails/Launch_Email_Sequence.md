**Placeholders** to fill before scheduling: `[FIRST NAME]` (with a fallback like "there"), `[STORE LINK]`, `[LAUNCH END DATE]`, `[POSTAL ADDRESS]`, `[UNSUBSCRIBE LINK]` (inserted by your email platform).

**Compliance footer (every email).** The U.S. CAN-SPAM Act requires accurate sender details, a subject line that reflects the content, a valid physical postal address, a clear way to opt out, and opt-outs honored within 10 business days (FTC, "CAN-SPAM Act: A Compliance Guide for Business"). Your email platform adds the unsubscribe link. You must supply the postal address (a registered PO box or private mailbox is acceptable under the FTC guide). If you have subscribers in the UK or EU, you also need their consent, which the signup form records.

```text
You're receiving this because you signed up for the Band of One email at [SITE].
Band of One · [POSTAL ADDRESS] · Unsubscribe: [UNSUBSCRIBE LINK]
```
